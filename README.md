## Installation

1. Open [TUNTAS.apk](https://github.com/DemonPalace/TuntasAplication/releases/latest/download/TUNTAS.apk) on your phone and download it.
2. Tap the file to install.
3. If a warning appears, allow **Install from unknown sources** for your browser.

## Features

- **Four books, one per subject:** Mathematics (12 chapters), Physics (13 chapters), Indonesian (10 chapters), and English (10 chapters). Each chapter includes worked examples revealed step by step, a list of common pitfalls, and a summary.
- **Math and Physics formulas:** beautifully rendered in LaTeX, both in the books and in the questions.
- **English book written in English,** just like the real exam, with short translations for key parts.
- **Study map:** every subtopic is a node, with a practice session at the end of each chapter. Nodes unlock one at a time as you progress.
- **512 questions** (multiple choice, complex multiple choice, and categorized complex multiple choice), each with an answer key and full explanation.
- **Progress tracking:** levels, daily streaks, a daily chart, and a list of questions you still get wrong.
- **Daily study sessions:** study, do the questions, then snap a photo or type a short summary. The screen stays on throughout the session.
- **Dark mode** for comfortable studying at any hour.

## Sources

Topic coverage follows the 2026 Senior High School Exam (Ujian SMA 2026) blueprint from Pusmendik. Some questions come from official sample items; others were written in-house, modeled on examples from detikEdu, CNN Indonesia, Kompas.com, and ITERA Blog. The full list is available in the app under **Progress → Sources**.
